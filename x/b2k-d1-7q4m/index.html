ABOUT

BACK IN THE 2000s: THE GAME
Story, characters and dialogue from the book by the author.
Game built with Claude.

Chris is a high-schooler in Belleville, Illinois in 2003 with a job
pushing carts at Schnucks, three dogs and friends with terrible ideas.

Play the story missions (yellow markers), then live in the town:
work shifts, rent movies, skate, hang out, make curfew.
